Bisaya / Visayas
Cariñosa
Sayaw ng Pag-ibig · Courtship Dance
Born in the Spanish colonial era, the Cariñosa (meaning affectionate or lovable) is considered the national folk dance of the Philippines. It is a courtship dance filled with flirtatious hide-and-seek gestures using a fan and handkerchief, symbolizing the shyness and charm of Filipino romance.

Lubang Island, Mindoro
Pandanggo sa Ilaw
Sayaw ng Ilaw · Dance of Lights
Originating from Lubang Island in Mindoro, Pandanggo sa Ilaw (Fandango of Lights) is one of the most visually stunning Filipino folk dances. Derived from the Spanish fandango, it showcases the dancer's exceptional balance and grace as they move with three oil lamps — one on the head and one balanced on the back of each hand.

Bohol / Eastern Visayas
Kuratsa
Sayaw ng Pagdiriwang · Celebration Dance
The Kuratsa is a lively courtship and celebration dance from Bohol in the Eastern Visayas. It is traditionally performed at weddings and fiestas, often as a highlight of the reception. The dance tells a universal story: a young man chases a woman who playfully resists, until they finally dance together in joyful celebration.
